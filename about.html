About SportZone

Who we are

SportZone is an online store for sports equipment. We sell gear for football, basketball, tennis, fitness and swimming, from beginners to club players.

This website was built as the final project for the INT424 course. Every product, price and stock level on the site is loaded live from a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, and customer orders are saved there as well.

How it works

Browse & search

Search by keywords and filter by category, price range and availability.

Cart & checkout

Your cart is kept in your browser. Create an account to check out.

Track orders

See your orders in My Account and cancel them while they are still pending.
